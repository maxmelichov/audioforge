- Date: 2026-09-30
- Source: research/TURN_V5.md, "## Ablations" table (rows a_pool, c2, a_big) and the "Capacity" bullet.
- Budget: equal steps, not equal wall-clock: 3000 steps, batch 256, lr 1e-4, dropout 0.2.

- Eval metric: val AUC on the turn_v4 held-out 8 % (all frames / first 320 ms), calls / AMI quiet-frame AUC, smart-turn
