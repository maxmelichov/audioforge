# Capacity record: language ID head (LanguageHead `hidden`), both cores

Recorded from existing measurements, not from `scripts/sweep_capacity.py`.

- Date: 2026-10-02 (FIXALL step 3), with the 256 vs 512 step from 2026-09-29 (research/archive/LID.md fix pass)
- Sources:
  - research/FIXALL.md, "### 3. Language ID", candidates table; runs/fixall.json `ltrain` (tags fx_amb_h512,
    fx_amb_h1024, fx6_amb_h512, fx6_amb_h1024).
  - research/archive/LID.md, the kd table (`kd_a8` hidden 256 vs `kd_h512` hidden 512).
- Budget: equal steps, not equal wall-clock: 8000 steps, batch 128, the dev split picks the step. Wall-clock grew
  with size (115M: 1442 s for 512, 2439 s for 1024).
- Eval metric: FLEURS-17 dev accuracy at 2 s and on the full clip (selection split; test touched once per shipped head).
  No eval loss was logged.
- Other sizes held fixed: att_hidden 128, cls_hidden 256 (never swept).

115M (blocks 8-12, AmberNet teacher, alpha 0.8):

| hidden | params | data | dev 2 s | dev full | source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 256 | – | train + extra English | 89.2 | 97.2 | archive/LID.md `kd_a8` |
| 512 | 0.92 M | train + extra English | 91.2 | 97.5 | archive/LID.md `kd_h512` (= `lid_distill.pt`) |
| 512 | 0.92 M | train + trainx + extra English | 91.0 | 97.2 | FIXALL `fx_amb_h512` |
| **1024** | 2.37 M | train + trainx + extra English | **93.1** | **98.2** | FIXALL `fx_amb_h1024` (shipped, `lid_115m_v2.pt`) |

0.6B (blocks 16 / 20, AmberNet teacher, + trainx):

| hidden | params | dev 2 s | dev full | source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 512 | 1.19 M | 92.6 | 97.7 | FIXALL `fx6_amb_h512` |
| **1024** | 2.89 M | **94.1** | 97.6 | FIXALL `fx6_amb_h1024` (shipped, `lid_0p6b_v2.pt`) |

**Pick:** hidden 1024 on both cores (+2.1 / +1.5 points at 2 s over 512, no measurable latency cost).

Caveat: 1024 is the largest size tried, so the knee is not bracketed upward. The rows mix two data sets (256 vs 512 on
the archive data, 512 vs 1024 with trainx). A proper sweep would add 1536 / 2048 at equal wall-clock on one data set.
